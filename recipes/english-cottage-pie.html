English Cottage Pie

This cottage pie has a flavorful layer of savory ground beef and veggies topped with fluffy mashed potato and cheese for a comforting dinner the whole family will love!

Ingriedients

  • 1 Pound Lean Gound Beef
  • 1 Onion Diced
  • 3 Carrots Diced
  • 2 Tablespoons Flour
  • 1 1/2 Cups Beef Broth
  • 1 Tablespoon Tomato Paste
  • 4 Potatoes, Peeled and Diced
  • 1 Cup Milk
  • 1/2 Cup Butter
  • 1/4 Pound Shredded Cheddar Cheese
